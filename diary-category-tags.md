# A diary entry takes square-bracket category tags

A diary entry may be categorized with square-bracket tags, as in `[idea]`. A tag applies to a
whole entry or to one bullet point.

- **Bullet point:** the tag prefixes the bullet's text — `- [idea] My great idea is thus`.
- **Whole entry:** the tag goes on a dedicated `**Tags:**` line, placed directly under the
  datetime line and above the `**Summary:**` line where there is one. Several tags are
  separated by spaces — `**Tags:** [idea] [decision]`.

Tags are freeform and lower-case. A multiword tag is dash-separated and lower-case, as
`[legal-matter]` is. There is no fixed controlled vocabulary, and a tag is introduced as it is
needed.

**Why:** it is a light, skimmable categorization. It is visible in a raw-text Quick Look
preview, it is found by an ordinary text search, and it imposes no rigid taxonomy.

**How to apply:** where the writer prefixes dictated text with a `[tag]`, preserve it verbatim.
To categorize a whole entry, add a `**Tags:**` line under the datetime line. Where the writer
states a categorization in passing, tag accordingly.
