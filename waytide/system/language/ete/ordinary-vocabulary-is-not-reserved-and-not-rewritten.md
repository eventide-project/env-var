# Ordinary vocabulary is not reserved and not rewritten

**The STE dictionary refuses hundreds of the words any English prose runs on.** Waytide's own rule
files use `as` 411 times, `every` 221, `never` 133, `under` 126, `would` 121, `already` 105, and
`may` 86. The dictionary gives BECAUSE, ALL, DO NOT, BELOW, CAN, IN PROGRESS, and CAN for them.

**None of them is a technical noun or a technical verb, in any category.** A technical noun names a
thing in a subject field, and a technical verb names an action performed in one. `every` names
nothing. `under` names nothing. No category holds them, and ETE's two do not either.

**So the two dispositions ETE has both fail.** Reserving them would put a false claim in a
vocabulary, in the form conformance takes, which is worse than the divergence it hides. Rewriting
them reaches every sentence in the corpus, for a word that carries no meaning of its own.

**ETE conforms its terminology and does not conform its ordinary vocabulary.** **This contradicts
STE Rule 1.1**, which admits an approved word, a technical noun, and a technical verb, and nothing
else.

**The line between the two is what a word names.** A word that names something in the subject field
is terminology, and it is reserved or it is rewritten. A word that does the sentence's grammatical
work, or joins two statements, or qualifies one, is ordinary vocabulary. `branch` is terminology.
`under` is not.

**The divergence stays visible.** The unclaimed-words check reports every refused word the prose
uses, and it does not sort them by this line, because no check can. A reader reads the report and
sees both.

**Why:** the terminology is the conveyable output of this work, and it is what a reader has to learn
to use the method. A term that misleads costs the reader the concept. `every` written where the
dictionary gives ALL costs nothing, and the reader is not misled by either.

The trade is not close. Conforming the terminology is bounded work with a real return. Conforming
the ordinary vocabulary is unbounded work, and its return is a claim of conformance rather than a
reader who understands more.

**How to apply:** ask what a refused word names. Reserve it in a category where it names something
in the subject field. Rewrite the prose where an approved word says the same thing. Leave it where
it is ordinary vocabulary, and record no entry for it.
