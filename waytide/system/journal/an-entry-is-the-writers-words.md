# An entry is the writer's words

**Do not write a journal entry.** Not on your own reading of what the session was about, and not
because a stretch of work seems worth recording. The agent has four records for that already.
The journal is the one the writer keeps.

**Where the writer dictates an entry, write down what they said.** Write their words, in their
order. Preserve their meaning and their emphasis. Correct the grammar and the flow, and go no
further than that. Invent no fact, and embellish nothing.

**What the agent adds is the entry's settled shape and nothing else.** That is the datetime
prefix and the `Authored by` line the journal-convention requires, the datetime line under the
title, a `**Tags:**` line where the writer gave tags, and the `**Summary:**` line the
journal-entries rule asks for. The summary states the entry's gist in the writer's own terms. It
is not the agent's reading of what the entry means, and it is dropped where it would repeat
the body.

**Do not edit an entry that is already there.** Not to correct a fact, not to fix a spelling,
and not to reconcile it with something that has since changed. An entry is a historical record.
The foundation package's disclosure rule governs the one exception.

**Do not offer to write one.** Naming the journal as a place to put something is the agent
reaching for a record that is not its own. Where the writer wants an entry, they say so.

**Why:** the journal is the writer's thinking, and thinking written by somebody else is not
thinking. An agent-written entry would read exactly like an writer-written one, and every
later reader would take it for the writer's. The record would then be untrustworthy at the
one thing it is for.

The other four records survive being the agent's work because they report what happened. The
journal reports nothing. It holds a judgment, and a judgment has an author or it has nothing.

**How to apply:** write no entry of your own. Where the writer dictates one, write their words,
correcting the grammar and the flow and no more. Add the prefix, the footer, the datetime line,
any tags they gave, and the summary. Change no entry that exists. Offer nothing.
